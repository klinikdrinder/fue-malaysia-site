An expertly executed walkthrough of FUE hair transplantation

Follicular Unit Extraction (FUE) is a minimally invasive hair transplant. Individual follicles are taken from a donor area, typically the back and sides of the head, and placed into thinning or balding areas. The method uses a specialised punch device, 0.6 mm to 1.0 mm in diameter, to extract follicular units complete with their necessary glands, meaning minimal trauma and better results. It is performed under high-powered loupes with specially designed instruments, for natural-looking results. Because there is no linear scar, you can keep your hair very short afterwards; FUE leaves only tiny dot scars that are usually hidden by hair.

Step 02 · Removing and preparing hair follicles

Individual follicular units are removed by making a small circular incision around each unit and extracting it with a tweezer-like instrument. Trained technicians then inspect the grafts under high-powered stereo microscopes and prepare them for transplantation. This meticulous, precise process helps ensure the best results.

Step 03 · Placing the follicular units

The prepared grafts are inserted into the recipient area through small incisions that match your natural hair growth pattern. They are placed one by one, taking into account the number of hairs in each follicle, to maximise naturalness, density and coverage of the thinning areas. The success of the procedure depends largely on the experience and skill of the FUE doctor.

Step 04 · FUE healing and hair growth

The small incisions made during extraction heal completely within a few days. New hair growth usually starts at around three months, with noticeable regrowth at about six months and full results at 6–12 months. From then on, both your native and transplanted hair continue to grow naturally.
