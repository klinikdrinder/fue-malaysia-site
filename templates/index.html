What to expect

Your new hair, month by month.

Hair grows slowly, so knowing the timeline helps. This is how most patients progress.

Day 1

Procedure day

Usually completed in one day, under local anaesthesia. You go home the same evening.

Week 1

Quick healing

The tiny openings heal and most people are back to daily life within a few days.

Weeks 2–8

Normal shedding

Transplanted hairs fall out. This is expected: the roots stay and rest.

Months 3–4

New growth

Fine new hairs start to appear in the treated area.

Month 6

Visible change

Density is clearly noticeable and the hair starts to thicken.

Months 6–12

Full result

Your new hairline matures, growing naturally for life.

Your questions, answered.

Ask on WhatsApp

The procedure is done under local anaesthesia. Most patients feel the anaesthetic injections and then only mild, manageable discomfort during and after treatment.

Most patients return to normal daily activities within a few days. For about a month you avoid strenuous exercise, swimming pools, the sea and direct sun on the scalp.

Transplanted hair usually sheds in the first weeks. New growth starts at around 3 to 4 months, is clearly noticeable by 6 months, and the fuller result appears between 6 and 12 months.

Follicles are taken from the donor area at the back and sides of the head, which is naturally resistant to hair loss, so transplanted hair keeps growing for life. Your existing hair can still thin over time, which is why Dr Inder may also recommend medical treatment.

The cost depends mainly on how many grafts you need and which areas are treated. You receive a personal plan and quotation after your free consultation with Dr Inder.

Datuk Dr Inder designs your hairline and leads your procedure from start to finish, supported by her trained clinical team.
